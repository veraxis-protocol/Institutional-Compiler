# CDC-END-TO-END-MISSION-001 — EXPERIMENTAL INTEGRITY AND OBSERVABILITY DOCTRINE v0.1

## Status

OWNER-FROZEN PRE-RESULT DOCTRINE

Effective before any result-bearing Stage-2 execution.

Assurance ceiling:

`SYNTHETIC_EVALUATION_ONLY`

This doctrine governs how observations from CDC-END-TO-END-MISSION-001 may be produced, classified, continued, and reported. It does not alter the frozen oracle, protocol, mission inputs, action plan, implementation, or previously recorded observations.

## 1. Observability, not completeness

Completeness of the measured-case denominator is not an experimental objective.

A preregistered case becomes measurable only when its frozen preconditions arise from:

1. already-frozen mission inputs;
2. already-frozen semantic and adjudication rules;
3. already-frozen stimuli;
4. the authorized execution of those objects;

without a new act introduced after the relevant freeze point for the purpose of obtaining a desired observation or classification.

Accordingly:

**No action may be taken for the purpose of converting an otherwise valid `INCOMPLETE_OBSERVATION` into `MATCH`.**

Equally:

**No action may be taken for the purpose of converting an observed `SEMANTIC_VIOLATION` or `FORBIDDEN_PROMOTION` into `INCOMPLETE_OBSERVATION`, `NONCOMPARABLE`, or any other non-evaluative state. Once a violation has been validly observed, it remains an observed violation in that run's immutable record.**

Observed failure may be corrected prospectively. It may not be erased retrospectively.

## 2. Meaning of a prerequisite arising under the frozen experiment

Because this mission is synthetic, “arising naturally” does not mean “not designed.”

For this mission, a prerequisite has arisen within the experiment only when it is produced from already-frozen inputs, rules, and stimuli through the already-authorized execution path, without a new post-freeze act introduced for the purpose of causing that prerequisite to exist.

A prerequisite is not considered to have arisen merely because it could be constructed, simulated, injected, or produced through a rerun.

## 3. Classification authority

The owner does not assign `MATCH`, `SEMANTIC_VIOLATION`, `FORBIDDEN_PROMOTION`, `INCOMPLETE_OBSERVATION`, `NONCOMPARABLE`, or another adjudication state.

Those classifications belong to the designated adjudicator operating under the frozen oracle and adjudication protocol.

The owner may:

- authorize execution;
- freeze evidence;
- accept an independently adjudicated result;
- authorize a separately identified successor observation.

The owner may not request, direct, condition, or structure an execution for the purpose of obtaining a particular adjudication state.

In particular:

**The owner must not request a desired `MATCH`, `PASS`, `INCOMPLETE`, or other protocol classification from the adjudicator.**

## 4. Immutable predecessor observations

Every completed observation remains immutable as the observation that actually occurred.

A later observation may:

- extend coverage;
- exercise a previously unobserved branch;
- test a previously unavailable prerequisite;
- measure a previously incomplete property;
- demonstrate correction of a previously observed defect.

A later observation may not rewrite, replace, or retrospectively improve the classification of the predecessor run.

The precedent is the Slice-001 missing-measurement treatment: the predecessor RUN-002 remained permanently `INCOMPLETE`; later measurement was represented through a separately identified successor aggregate rather than by rewriting RUN-002.

Therefore an `INCOMPLETE_OBSERVATION` in CDC-END-TO-END-MISSION-001 does not prohibit later measurement.

It requires that any later measurement be:

1. separately authorized;
2. separately identified;
3. separately evidenced;
4. independently adjudicated;
5. linked as a successor observation;
6. incapable of altering the historical predecessor record.

## 5. No completeness-driven rerun

The mission must not be rerun, altered, extended, or supplied with additional post-freeze stimuli merely to increase:

- measured-case count;
- MATCH count;
- apparent completion;
- submission attractiveness;
- aggregate status.

If a frozen prerequisite does not occur, the corresponding case remains `INCOMPLETE_OBSERVATION` for that run.

If a frozen prerequisite does occur and an observed result constitutes a violation, that violation remains part of that run.

Neither an approaching submission deadline nor the communicative advantage of a larger denominator changes these rules.

## 7. Negative observations and observed violations

The doctrine protects negative evidence with the same force as positive evidence.

If Stage 2 produces:

- a forbidden promotion;
- an unauthorized transition;
- a semantic violation;
- a corrupted predecessor;
- an improperly elevated epistemic state;
- another frozen protocol violation;

that observation must be frozen before any corrective action.

A later corrected run may demonstrate remediation.

It may not cause the predecessor violation to become unobserved.

## 8. Independent adjudicator role and disclosure

The independent adjudicator did not implement the mechanisms being evaluated and did not author the controlling semantic objects.

The adjudicator's role is to:

- recompute identities;
- independently apply frozen rules;
- classify observed evidence under the frozen protocol;
- identify where a proposed claim exceeds what was observed.

Where findings by the adjudicator exposed a seam or ambiguity and subsequently led to a new controlling artifact, that intervention must be disclosed.

In Mission 001, the adjudicator identified the `on_unknown` seam during review 001. The resulting seam clarification was an owner-issued governance act. The adjudicator did not author that controlling semantic artifact and did not participate in implementation.

Accordingly:

**Where adjudicator findings led to the issuance of later artifacts, those artifacts remain explicit owner acts; the adjudicator neither authored controlling semantic objects nor implemented the mechanisms later adjudicated.**

This disclosure is part of the independence record rather than an exception to it.

## 10. Reporting rule

External reporting must preserve the denominator and the reason for incompleteness.

If, for example, eleven of twelve preregistered properties become measurable and all eleven MATCH while the twelfth prerequisite does not occur, the accurate formulation is:

> Within the `SYNTHETIC_EVALUATION_ONLY` frozen execution, eleven of twelve preregistered continuity properties became observable and all eleven matched their frozen criteria. The twelfth was left unscored because its preregistered prerequisite did not occur. No rerun or scenario modification was permitted to manufacture observability.

It must not be represented as:

- “11 of 12 passed”;
- “one test failed”;
- “12/12” through a later substituted observation;
- production conformance;
- CDC acceptance;
- legal validity;
- institutional authority.

## 11. Governing principle

**A run designed so that every case conveniently becomes measurable is weaker evidence than a run permitted to remain incomplete.**

The purpose of this mission is not to produce 12/12.

The purpose is to determine what the frozen architecture actually demonstrates under the frozen experiment, including the possibility of incompleteness or failure.

`OBSERVATION ≠ ADJUDICATION ≠ OWNER CLAIM DECISION`

and:

`PREDECESSOR OBSERVATION ≠ SUCCESSOR OBSERVATION`
