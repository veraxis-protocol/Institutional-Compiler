# Capability Roadmap

## 7-day experimental vertical slice

- Governance and contract freeze.
- One bounded public/synthetic procurement corpus.
- Document ingestion and source anchors.
- Candidate normative extraction.
- Prioritized ambiguity docket.
- Manual admission record.
- Open Control Envelope.
- Rego compilation and local OPA evaluation.
- ALLOW / DENY / CANNOT with source lineage.
- Published failures, raw outputs, environment, and limitations.

## 30-day reproducible alpha

- Frozen preflight benchmark subset.
- Repeatable local and hosted builds.
- ZTL and VEIP provisional adapter dossiers.
- Source-version change-impact demonstration.
- External technical review.
- Signed release, SBOM, provenance.

## 60-day evidence release

- Full OIC-Bench v0.1.
- Named reproducible baselines.
- Practitioner usability study.
- Hosted Lab alpha.
- Evidence-qualified claims only.

## 90-day enterprise pilot candidate

- Hardened security and tenancy.
- On-prem and air-gapped profile.
- Enterprise identity integration.
- Operational monitoring and rollback.
- Pilot-specific corpus and runtime integration.
