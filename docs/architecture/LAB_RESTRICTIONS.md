# Hosted Lab Launch Restrictions

Before upload, the Lab must display:

- Use only public, synthetic, or non-confidential samples.
- Do not upload privileged, personal, regulated, export-controlled, classified, or trade-secret material.
- Uploaded content is not used for model training.
- Retention and deletion periods must be stated.
- Candidate output is not approved policy, legal advice, or a compliance determination.
- Runtime artifacts remain experimental until an authorized reviewer admits the interpretation.
