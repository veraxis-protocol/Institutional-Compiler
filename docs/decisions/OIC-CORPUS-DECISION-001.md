# OIC-CORPUS-DECISION-001
## Canada Federal Procurement Preflight Selection

Decision owner:
Arkadiy Miteiko / Veraxis

Decision:

The Canada federal procurement candidate is selected for the bounded OIC
seven-day procurement preflight.

This decision selects a jurisdiction and source family. It does not complete
rights review, provenance, acquisition, annotation, or semantic admission.

## 1. Two Corpus Boundaries

### A. Acquisition Set

Complete official artifacts may be acquired where required to preserve:

- source identity;
- hierarchy;
- context;
- effective dates;
- amendment and supersession relationships;
- archive status;
- reproducibility.

### B. Benchmark Working Set

The seven-day semantic working set is limited to:

- 30–60 print-equivalent pages; or
- an equivalent bounded collection of precisely anchored HTML/XML source
  nodes.

The complete 305-page candidate pack is not authorized as the working set.

## 2. Selected Source Family

The bounded source family consists of:

### CA-1

Treasury Board Directive on the Management of Procurement:
only scope, definitions, authority, effective-date provisions, and sections
needed to interpret the selected appendices.

### CA-2

Appendix A — Contracting Approvals:
selected approval tables and their conditions.

### CA-3

Government Contracts Regulations SOR/87-402:
the complete current consolidated XML or PDF plus point-in-time metadata.

### CA-4

Appendix F — Mandatory Procedures for Business Owners When Procuring
Professional Services:
only the selected evidence, approval, record, and accountability provisions
used by the benchmark.

### CA-5

Buyer’s Guide:
only explicitly enumerated current pages corresponding to approval,
documentation, evaluation, award, and evidence requirements. No portal-wide
crawl is authorized.

### CA-6

Archived Supply Manual:
only the archive-status notice, selected Chapter 6 approval/authority
sections, and glossary entries directly referenced by the working set.

CA-7 is not part of the first working set unless the 60-page ceiling permits
its inclusion after all mandatory coverage is satisfied.

## 3. Public Approval Limits

Public Treasury Board approval tables are sufficient for this experimental
benchmark.

They are not treated as proof of an individual officer’s delegated authority.

Any transaction requiring unavailable department-specific or individual
delegation evidence must produce:

`epistemic_status = UNRESOLVED`

and later:

`execution_disposition = BLOCK or ESCALATE`

It must not produce a substantive ALLOW or substantive DENY.

## 4. Language Policy

The experimental working set is English-language only.

For every Canadian source, record the official French counterpart URL where
available.

The experiment must disclose that it does not test bilingual semantic
equivalence and does not claim to represent the complete bilingual
institutional meaning of Canadian federal instruments.

No English-only interpretation may be represented as dispositive Canadian
legal interpretation.

## 5. Rights and Redistribution

The preliminary rights basis is the Open Government Licence — Canada, subject
to source-level confirmation, attribution, and exclusions.

Before any source bytes are committed:

- verify the licence notice;
- screen third-party material;
- screen personal information;
- screen crests, logos, insignia, and trademarks;
- record attribution requirements;
- record any redistribution limitation.

Until that review passes, acquisition bytes remain outside the public Git
history. The repository may contain:

- acquisition scripts;
- URLs;
- response metadata;
- hashes;
- manifests;
- rights findings.

## 6. Acquisition Freeze

Every source receives its actual retrieval timestamp.

The corpus freeze timestamp is established by the acquisition PR.

No artifact retrieved after that freeze may enter v0.1 without an explicit
manifest amendment and recorded reason.

## 7. Non-Authorization

This decision does not authorize:

- LLM extraction;
- policy interpretation;
- normative annotation;
- institutional admission;
- Institutional IR;
- Open Control Envelope generation;
- ZTL execution;
- VEIP execution;
- Rego;
- runtime decisions;
- STATUS.md modification.

The semantic implementation gate remains BLOCKED.
