## Why this office

copyright/reproduction administration point for Department of Justice Canada
materials. The same official surface recognizes the Reproduction of Federal Law
Order, and the Justice Laws Website separately states that federal Acts and
regulations may be reproduced under that Order.

The candidate is therefore institutionally closer to the exact CA-3 rights
question than generic outside counsel.
