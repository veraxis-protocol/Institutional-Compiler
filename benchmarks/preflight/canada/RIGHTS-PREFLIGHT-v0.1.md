## Preliminary findings

The selected pages are publicly reachable or indexed on official Government
of Canada services. Public availability does not establish institutional
authority, current effectiveness, reuse permission, or redistribution
permission.

Government of Canada terms pages provide the currently identified notice
route. They include conditions and exclusions relevant to third-party
copyright, official symbols, crests, logos, insignia, trademarks, personal
information, and some commercial uses. The Open Government Licence — Canada
is treated only as a possible preliminary reuse basis until each selected
source expressly supports that classification.

No registry record is classified as legally cleared or redistributable.
The official Government of Canada terms support non-commercial reproduction
subject to accuracy and attribution conditions, restrict commercial
redistribution without permission, identify possible third-party copyright,
and prohibit reproduction of official symbols without written authorization.
Those terms are supporting evidence, not a uniform licence finding for every
source unit.

## Required source-level screen

Before any source bytes can be proposed for Git history:

1. capture the exact page-level licence or terms notice;
2. identify third-party text, images, standards, and linked assets;
3. identify official symbols, crests, logos, insignia, and trademarks;
4. identify personal information or privacy restrictions;
5. record attribution requirements;
6. distinguish non-commercial reproduction from commercial redistribution;
7. record a reviewer and review date;
8. issue an explicit redistribution finding for the exact acquired bytes.
