## Scenario feasibility, not outcomes

The selected structures appear sufficient to design later transaction
scenarios labelled ALLOW, BLOCK, and UNRESOLVED, but this preflight assigns no
transaction result and interprets no provision.

Public approval tables may be represented as evidence. They are not proof of
an individual officer’s delegated authority. When department-specific or
individual delegation evidence is unavailable, the scenario boundary is:

**UNRESOLVED — operational BLOCK or ESCALATE.**

It must not be converted into a substantive ALLOW or DENY.
