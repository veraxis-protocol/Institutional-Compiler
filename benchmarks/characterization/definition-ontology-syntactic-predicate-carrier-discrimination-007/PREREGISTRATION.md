## Offline localization

IIR-027 literally contains both:

- `consider`
- `reconciliation`

The governing action expected by the frozen evaluator is therefore available
in the candidate span.

Candidate-span omission is not the supported explanation.

The remaining localized competition is between:

1. a clause-governing lexical verb; and
2. an embedded event nominalization.

The frozen Interpretation Ruleset defines `action` as the predicate the force
applies to, but does not itself specify how to discriminate those two
syntactic carriers.

## New bounded hypothesis

When both structures occur in the same admitted proposition, the existing
role descriptions underdetermine predicate-carrier selection.

Adding only a source-syntactic carrier constraint should restore the governing
IIR-027 action without creating new non-force defects.

This hypothesis was generated after observing the predecessor result.

It is therefore not an independent discovery or generalization claim.

## Arm A

`A_ROLE_GUIDED_B2`

Exact frozen Ontology 006 `B_ROLE_GUIDED_B2` request semantics.

No predecessor response is reused.

## Arm B

`B_SYNTAX_CARRIER_GUIDED_B2`

Exact Arm A plus one frozen experimental suffix:

> For the action slot, identify the lexical verbal head that functions as the
> clause's governing predicate. If an auxiliary or modal governs that lexical
> verb, the governed lexical verb remains the action candidate. Do not promote
> a noun or nominalized event inside that verb's complement to action merely
> because it denotes an activity. Keep such nominal material in the object or
> complement unless the literal clause structure itself makes it the governing
> predicate. This is a source-syntactic carrier rule only. Do not infer or emit
> normative force.

This text is experimental guidance only.

It is **not** registered into the production Interpretation Ruleset.
