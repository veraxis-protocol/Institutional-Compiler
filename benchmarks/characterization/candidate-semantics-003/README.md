# OIC-CANDIDATE-SEMANTICS-003 frozen corpus

This successor corpus characterizes the minimal pre-admission candidate contract:
`candidate_span` plus provisional `unit_type`. Its 26 source texts are carried unchanged
from Corpus 002 so the architectural before/after can be compared without rewriting the
001 or 002 evidence.

`material_span_groups` are conjunctive across groups and disjunctive within a group. They
measure whether returned candidate spans collectively retain preregistered material source
language. `candidate_span_bounds` are optional, specimen-specific maximum proposition
regions for bounded overreach observation. They do not impose a universal shortest-span
rule.

Candidate IDs in 003 are intentionally version-transition identities derived from the
exact candidate span, provisional unit type, caller-controlled source anchor, and the 003
schema tag. They need not equal IDs produced by the semantic-role candidate schema in 002.

The A–M metric meanings are versioned to 003. Historical 001/002 receipts keep their own
role-metric meaning and are not reinterpreted as if produced by this contract.

This characterization measures source-grounded candidate discovery under one frozen
corpus, implementation commit, provider, model, and run condition. It does not establish
semantic correctness, institutional admission, authority, enforceability, legal
interpretation, runtime readiness, production readiness, or independent validation.

`independent_validation_claim = FALSE`. This corpus is NOT SELF-ADJUDICATED. No admission
or authority boundary, Institutional IR, runtime evaluation, or live NVIDIA result is
implemented or claimed here.
