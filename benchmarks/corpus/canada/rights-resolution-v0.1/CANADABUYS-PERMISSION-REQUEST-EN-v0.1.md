## Permission requested

1. **Retrieval of the exact named URLs** — Retrieval of only the ten source URLs listed in this request, and of no other page on the site. No crawl, no link following, and no discovery of additional pages.

2. **Internal research use** — Retention and reading of the retrieved bytes inside Veraxis for research on machine-readable representation of institutional procurement rules.

3. **Automated retrieval by a named acquisition tool** — Retrieval performed by the Veraxis acquisition tool identified below, which sends a single GET per URL, identifies itself in its User-Agent, follows no links, and honours any rate limit or schedule you specify. Your robots.txt currently disallows all agents other than bingbot and Googlebot, which is why this permission is requested explicitly rather than assumed.

4. **Machine processing** — Parsing, indexing, hashing, comparison and structural analysis of the retrieved bytes.

5. **Internal storage** — Storage of the exact bytes in access-controlled internal systems that are not publicly accessible.

6. **Public-repository redistribution** — Committing the exact unmodified bytes to a publicly readable source repository, accompanied by attribution and an unofficial-copy notice. If this is not permitted, we ask that the remaining permissions be granted on an internal-only basis.

7. **Publication of derived, non-official artifacts** — Publication of structured, machine-readable artifacts derived from the sources, such as structural indexes and audit records. These are our own work product, are labelled unofficial, and are not presented as Government of Canada interpretations.

8. **Preservation and attribution** — Preservation of each source's complete title, issuing organization, official URL and retrieval date alongside every copy and every derived artifact, in whatever form you require.

9. **Logos, branding and Government of Canada identifiers** — We propose to exclude official symbols, including the Canada wordmark, the Arms of Canada and the flag symbol, from anything we publish, and to retain them only where they are inseparable from bytes held internally. Please confirm or direct otherwise.

10. **Retention and deletion obligations** — Any retention period, deletion obligation, or notification requirement you wish to impose. We can delete retrieved bytes and derived artifacts on request and confirm the deletion in writing.

## Our commitments

- We will not modify the meaning of any source. Bytes are preserved exactly as served.
- Source material will not be represented as an official Veraxis product.
- Derived artifacts will not be represented as official Government of Canada interpretations of any policy or regulation.
- Access controls and deletion commitments can be applied if you require them.
- Permission may be limited to internal-only use. If public redistribution is not permitted, we ask that you grant the remaining categories on an internal-only basis rather than decline the request as a whole.
