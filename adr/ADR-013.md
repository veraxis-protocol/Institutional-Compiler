# ADR-013: Warrant, Epistemic Status, and Execution Disposition

**Status:** Proposed
**Work order:** OIC-WO-002
**Architecture authority:** GPT-5.6 Thinking. **Final design authority:** Arkadiy Miteiko / Veraxis.
**Scope:** architecture, proposed schemas, and conformance fixtures only. No ZTL call, no VEIP call, no admission, no envelope generation, no Rego, no runtime execution. The semantic implementation gate remains **BLOCKED**.

---

## 1. Problem

`schemas/draft/runtime-evaluation.schema.json` records the outcome of an evaluation in a
single field:

```json
"verdict": { "enum": ["ALLOW", "DENY", "CANNOT"] }
```

One field is being asked to carry three different things at once:

1. what the admitted grounds establish about the claim;
2. what the runtime operationally did;
3. why it did it.

Those three come apart, and the place they come apart is exactly where the system is most
dangerous. Two examples that are not hypothetical:

**The measured ZTL case.** The dossier reports, against the pinned kernel:

```
judge("p & q", {"p": "T", "q": "Z"})
  -> verdict='F', grade='until-verification', disposition='OPEN', unverified=['q']
```

The raw verdict is `F` while the disposition is `OPEN`. An adapter that reads `verdict`
turns *"q has not been verified"* into *"the claim is false"*. That is a direct violation
of **I-03** (unknown never serialises as grounded false), and it will not surface in
ordinary testing, because it needs a genuinely undetermined case to reach the runtime.

**The `on_unknown = deny` case.** `schemas/draft/control-envelope.schema.json` already
permits `on_unknown: "deny"`. That is a reasonable *operational* posture — fail closed when
you do not know. But if the resulting record says `verdict: "DENY"` and nothing else, then
a fail-closed block and a grounds-established prohibition are byte-identical downstream.
Any consumer — an appeal, an audit, a change-impact analysis, a VEIP reliance record —
will read the second when only the first happened.

The failure mode in both cases is the same: **absence of verification is recorded as
established negation.** Once that record exists it propagates, and nothing downstream can
recover the distinction, because the distinguishing information was never written down.

## 2. Decision

Record the three things in three fields, and never let one stand in for another.

### 2.1 Terminology

**Ground.** An admitted proposition supplied *to* the kernel. Grounds arrive from OIC's
admission plane. The kernel does not read documents and does not decide what is admitted.

**Warrant artifact.** A recomputable record of what a logic kernel established over a
supplied ground set, bound to a pinned kernel, an input hash, a ground-set hash, and a
ground epoch. A warrant is *evidence*, not an assertion: `recomputation_reference` is
required so it can be re-derived rather than trusted.

**Warranty grade.** How durable the result is under further verification:
`hereditary` ⟹ `sound` ⟹ (weakest) `until-verification`. `hereditary` is invariant under
every admissible monotone refinement of the *same* formula and marking.

**Epistemic status.** What the admitted grounds establish about the claim.

**Execution disposition.** What the runtime did.

**Decision basis.** Why the runtime did that.

### 2.2 The three dimensions

**A — `epistemic_status`**

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ESTABLISHED` | The grounds establish the claim. |
| `REFUTED` | The grounds establish its negation. |
| `UNRESOLVED` | The grounds do not settle it, **or it was never evaluated**. |
| `CONTRADICTED` | The grounds are mutually inconsistent. |

`UNRESOLVED` is not a weak `REFUTED`. `CONTRADICTED` is not `REFUTED` either: inconsistent
grounds establish nothing about the claim, they establish something about the grounds.

**B — `execution_disposition`**

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ALLOW` | The action may proceed. |
| `BLOCK` | The action is stopped. |
| `ESCALATE` | Routed to a human or higher authority. |
| `ADVISORY` | Recorded, does not gate the action. |

**C — `decision_basis`**

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SUBSTANTIVE` | The grounds themselves determine the outcome. |
| `PRECAUTIONARY` | The epistemic state is unresolved and the system fails closed. |
| `PROCEDURAL` | An artifact, binding, or availability precondition failed; the claim was never evaluated. |

The `PRECAUTIONARY` / `PROCEDURAL` split is the one that answers *"could this have gone
differently?"*. `PRECAUTIONARY` means the logic ran and ground is genuinely missing —
verify the ground and re-run. `PROCEDURAL` means the logic never ran, or ran against the
wrong world — fix the plumbing and re-run. They lead to different remediation and must not
be merged.

### 2.3 The load-bearing invariants

**W-1.** ZTL `disposition = OPEN`, **whatever the raw verdict**, maps to
`epistemic_status = UNRESOLVED`. It MUST NEVER map to `REFUTED`.

**W-2.** `raw_verdict` is recorded for replay only and MUST NOT drive any decision.

**W-3.** A `PRECAUTIONARY` or `PROCEDURAL` block MUST NOT be serialised, reported, or
reused as an established negative fact or a grounded prohibition. Only
`SUBSTANTIVE` + (`REFUTED` | `CONTRADICTED`) is a statement about the grounds.

**W-4.** `execution_disposition = ALLOW` requires **every** condition below. Failing any
one yields `BLOCK` or `ESCALATE`, never `ALLOW`:

1. `epistemic_status = ESTABLISHED`;
2. a warrant with `warrant_state = USABLE`;
3. observed warranty grade at least the envelope's required grade;
4. formula hash matches the formula bound to the control;
5. ground epoch, source version set, and admission version all match;
6. no dependency ground expired or revoked;
7. kernel available, pinned, and version-matched;
8. authority, admission, evidence, and version checks **outside** the kernel all pass.

Condition 8 is not decorative. ZTL does not check authority or admission and says so
plainly; a warrant is necessary for `ALLOW` and nowhere near sufficient.

**W-5.** `missing_ground_ids` is populated whenever the kernel reported unverified
grounds — **including when the outcome is `ALLOW`**. A `hereditary` result can carry
unverified atoms and still be invariant; the reviewer still needs to see them.

**W-6.** `on_unknown = deny` produces `execution_disposition = BLOCK` with
`decision_basis` of `PRECAUTIONARY` or `PROCEDURAL`, **never** `SUBSTANTIVE`.

**W-7.** Unknown grade values (a kernel returning a grade OIC does not know) are
`UNSUPPORTED_GRADE` → `UNRESOLVED` / `PROCEDURAL`. Never optimistic, never guessed.

### 2.4 Failure semantics

Full table in [`docs/contracts/ZTL-OCE-MAPPING-v0.1.md`](../docs/contracts/ZTL-OCE-MAPPING-v0.1.md);
every row has a golden fixture. Summary of the required posture:

| Condition | Epistemic | Disposition | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warrant absent | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Kernel unavailable | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Malformed warrant | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Unverifiable warrant hash | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Stale warrant (ground set moved) | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Expired warrant (`valid_until` passed) | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Revoked ground | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PRECAUTIONARY` |
| Expired ground | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PRECAUTIONARY` |
| Epoch mismatch | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Source-version mismatch | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Admission-version mismatch | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Formula mismatch | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Missing source anchors | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Missing admission IDs | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| Unsupported warranty grade | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| `OPEN` (raw T, F, or Z alike) | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PRECAUTIONARY` |
| `REFUTED` | `REFUTED` | BLOCK | `SUBSTANTIVE` |
| Contradictory grounds | `CONTRADICTED` | BLOCK | `SUBSTANTIVE` |
| `EARNED` + `hereditary`, all checks pass | `ESTABLISHED` | ALLOW | `SUBSTANTIVE` |
| `EARNED` + `sound`, grade permitted | `ESTABLISHED` | ALLOW | `SUBSTANTIVE` |
| `EARNED` + `sound`, grade insufficient | `ESTABLISHED` | ESCALATE | `PROCEDURAL` |
| `EARNED` + `until-verification` | `UNRESOLVED` | BLOCK / ESCALATE | `PRECAUTIONARY` |
| Unverified grounds non-empty | recorded, never suppressed | per grade | per grade |

Whether BLOCK or ESCALATE applies is chosen by the envelope's `on_unknown`
(`cannot`/`deny` → BLOCK, `escalate` → ESCALATE) and its `decision_mode`. Neither choice
changes the epistemic status.

Two rows deserve comment.

*`EARNED` + `sound` with insufficient grade* is `ESTABLISHED` / `ESCALATE` / `PROCEDURAL`.
The grounds do establish the claim — the epistemic status is honest about that. What
failed is a policy precondition the envelope set, not the logic. Calling this
`UNRESOLVED` would misreport the logic; calling it `SUBSTANTIVE` would misreport the
reason.

*`EARNED` + `until-verification`* is `UNRESOLVED`, not `ESTABLISHED`. A result that holds
only in the present marking has not established anything durable. If the kernel can emit
this combination at all, it must not reach `ALLOW`.

### 2.5 Missing grounds: two levels

`missing_ground_ids` — stable atom or proposition identifiers, exactly as the kernel emits
them. Level 1.

`missing_ground_anchors` — human-review references linking each missing proposition to
source document, source node or span, admitted normative unit, and expected evidence type.
Level 2.

The kernel emits level 1 only. Enrichment to level 2 is the OIC adapter's job, when the
adapter is eventually authorised. **The kernel is never asked to interpret source
documents** — that would breach the ZTL boundary (ADR-009) in the direction most likely to
look helpful. An empty `missing_ground_anchors` means enrichment has not run; it does not
mean there are no missing grounds. This answers the `MissingGround` granularity question
the ZTL side raised.

## 3. ZTL boundary

ZTL **ends** with: logical disposition; warranty grade; formula and dependency
information; verified and unverified grounds; epoch and freshness information;
recomputation evidence.

ZTL does **not**: authenticate a source; determine authority, delegation, or jurisdiction;
interpret prose; create admission; decide ALLOW/BLOCK/ESCALATE; create a VEIP lifecycle
record.

OIC consumes `disposition`, `warranty_grade`, and `unverified` as the operational triple.
`raw_verdict` is an internal kernel detail recorded for replay (W-2).

`hereditary` is absorbing under monotone refinement **and only under it**. It does not
waive re-checking on expiry, revocation, correction, source invalidation, schema change,
formula change, semantic-version change, or institutional admissibility. Treating it as
"settled forever" is, in the ZTL side's own words, the most attractive wrong reading of
the warranty ladder, and W-4 conditions 4–8 exist to prevent it.

Expiry is **scoped**, never global: `valid_until` is per-artifact and
`revocation_references` are per-ground. Unrestricted expiry makes every warranty invariant
trivially true, which would turn the whole apparatus into decoration.

## 4. VEIP boundary

VEIP **begins** with: consequential action proposal; binding to the admitted control and
authority version; runtime execution disposition; execution occurrence; evidence
recording; reliance; revocation propagation; correction.

`RuntimeDecision` is the handoff record: it is the last artifact OIC produces before VEIP
lifecycle begins, and the first artifact VEIP binds to. **ZTL must not create a VEIP
lifecycle record. VEIP must not reinterpret the ZTL formula.** A VEIP implementation that
re-derives a decision from `formula` rather than consuming `execution_disposition` has
crossed back over the boundary.

VEIP semantics and lifecycle commitments are **not** settled by this ADR. Arkadiy Miteiko
is interim VEIP contract owner (OIC-OWNER-DECISION-002 §5); this ADR defines only the
shape of the handoff, and defers the lifecycle to OIC-GC-004.

## 5. Invariant preservation

| Invariant | How this contract preserves it |
|---|---|
| **I-02** no enforcement artifact from a pre-`admitted` state | `admission_ids` required on the warrant; missing admission IDs is a blocking `PROCEDURAL` failure |
| **I-03** unknown never serialises as grounded false | W-1, W-2, W-3, W-6; `UNRESOLVED` is a distinct value and `raw_verdict` is inert |
| **I-04** missing authority/admission/evidence/version/grounds never silently yields ALLOW | W-4, all eight conditions; every failure path lands on BLOCK or ESCALATE |
| **I-05** extraction confidence never substitutes for admission | no confidence field exists anywhere in either proposed schema |
| **I-07** executable artifacts bind versions | `kernel_commit`, `ground_epoch`, `ground_set_hash`, `authority_version`, `admission_version`, `source_version_set_hash` |
| **I-13** revoked/expired authority cannot yield a current allow | revoked and expired grounds are blocking; `valid_until` and `revocation_references` are required fields |
| **I-14** changes create new versions, never silent mutation | epoch and ground-set hash mismatch force recomputation rather than reuse |
| **I-15** every verdict replayable | `recomputation_reference`, `input_hash`, `output_hash`, `replay_reference` |

## 6. Compatibility impact and migration

**Nothing in `schemas/draft/` is modified by this ADR.** The proposals live in
`schemas/proposed/` and are inert until an owner decision admits them. `STATUS.md` is
unchanged and the semantic gate remains BLOCKED.

Three migrations are *proposed*, not applied:

1. **`runtime-evaluation.schema.json` → `runtime-decision.schema.json`.** The single
   `verdict` field is replaced by the three dimensions. The mapping from the old field is
   lossy in one direction only, and that is the point: `DENY` cannot be reconstructed into
   `REFUTED`-versus-fail-closed, because the old schema never recorded which it was. Any
   migration must therefore treat historical `DENY` records as **indeterminate**, not as
   `SUBSTANTIVE`. Back-filling them would manufacture evidence.
2. **`control-envelope.schema.json` needs a minimum warranty grade.** No field expresses
   "this control requires a `hereditary` warrant" today, so the permitted-versus-insufficient
   grade distinction cannot be represented. Proposed addition:
   `minimum_warranty_grade: "hereditary" | "sound" | "until-verification"`, defaulting to
   `hereditary` for `decision_mode: automatic`. **Not applied here** — `schemas/draft` is
   Class B and out of scope for this work order.
3. **`on_unknown` gains a documented reading rather than a new value.** `deny` stays, but
   the contract fixes it as an operational fail-closed block only (W-6). No schema change
   is needed; the change is that a conforming implementation must now also record
   `decision_basis`, which makes the distinction visible.

## 7. Rejected alternatives

**Keep one `verdict` field and add a reason code.** Rejected: reason codes are advisory in
practice and get dropped by consumers, while the enum survives. The distinction between
"unverified" and "false" is too load-bearing to carry in an optional-feeling field. It
also leaves `DENY` as the literal serialised value for a merely unknown state, which is
what I-03 forbids.

**Add `UNKNOWN` to the existing verdict enum.** Rejected: it still conflates epistemic
state with execution. `UNKNOWN` would have to mean both "we do not know" and "therefore we
blocked", and a control that escalates on unknown could not be distinguished from one that
blocks.

**Use ZTL's `T`/`F`/`Z` directly as the OIC epistemic status.** Rejected on the ZTL side's
own advice. `Z` is a mark on verification status rather than a third truth value, compound
formulas collapse it, and the raw verdict can be `F` while the disposition is `OPEN`.
Adopting the raw marking would import exactly the confusion W-1 exists to prevent.

**Let the kernel emit clause-level missing-ground anchors.** Rejected: it would require
the kernel to interpret source documents, breaching ADR-009. Two levels, with enrichment
on the OIC side, keeps the boundary intact.

**Treat `hereditary` as permanently settled and skip revalidation.** Rejected as measured
error: `hereditary` is absorbing under monotone refinement only, and says nothing about
expiry, revocation, or correction.

**Define expiry globally.** Rejected as measured error: from any marking `{expire, verify}`
reaches every marking, so a property invariant under refinement *and* arbitrary expiry is
constant — a frame that cannot fail, and a test that proves nothing.
