# Current Limitations

- This is a governance and contract scaffold, not a functioning compiler.
- The first vertical slice will support a bounded public or synthetic procurement corpus, not arbitrary enterprise policy.
- Candidate extraction will not constitute institutional admission.
- Open-textured standards remain human-judgment boundaries unless an admitted decision procedure exists.
- Cross-jurisdictional authority precedence is not automated.
- The ZTL adapter is provisional until Vitaliy Reznik's interface and verification dossier is frozen.
- The VEIP adapter is provisional until a pinned public interface and conformance dossier is frozen.
- The hosted Lab must initially reject confidential, privileged, regulated, export-controlled, and classified content.
- Rego is the first executable target. Other targets do not imply semantic parity until tested.
- Benchmark thresholds are preregistered targets or gates, not measured results.
